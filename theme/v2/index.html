FIELD_NOTE_001.TXT

Lake Superior agate

The only red rock on the beach that will let a little daylight through.

  • Banded chalcedony that filled gas bubbles in lava about 1.1 billion years ago.
  • Iron gives the reds, oranges, and yellows.
  • Minnesota's state gemstone since 1969.
  • Lookalikes: red jasper and chert are opaque and have no bands.

Sources: Minnesota DNR; Minnesota Secretary of State. Agate Expectations tee

FIELD_NOTE_002.TXT

Petoskey or Charlevoix?

Same reef, same sea, smaller apartments.

  • Petoskey: the coral Hexagonaria. Larger cells, each with a dark eye and lines radiating out.
  • Charlevoix: the coral Favosites. Smaller, finer cells, usually no center dot.
  • Both come from Devonian rocks. Michigan made the Petoskey its state stone in 1965.

Sources: University of Michigan Museum of Paleontology; Michigan Legislature. Petoskey socks

FIELD_NOTE_003.TXT

Leland Blue is slag

Michigan's most beloved rock is a 150-year-old industrial accident.

  • Glassy waste from an iron furnace in Leland, Michigan, that started in 1870.
  • Look for mixed-size gas bubbles, glassy breaks, and rusty spots.
  • Blue beach glass is even in color and thickness. Leland Blue is lumpy and streaky.

Sources: Fishtown Preservation; 9&10 News.

FIELD_NOTE_004.TXT

How to look

Head down. Sun in front. Thumb wet.

  • Walk the wave line, or carry a spray bottle.
  • Face the sun: translucent stones light up when it shines through them toward you.
  • Go after a storm, when big waves turn over the gravel.
  • Pick beaches with lots of pebbles, not pure sand.

Sources: Minnesota DNR; Michigan EGLE. Will brake for agates tee

ABOUT.TXT

Pockets, explained

Heavy Pockets Co. makes tees and socks for people who walk the Great Lakes shoreline looking down. If you have ever wet a pebble with your thumb to check it, said "one more minute" an hour ago, or heard your jacket clank on the way to the car, we draw for you.

It is run by Brian, a rockhound in Grand Rapids, Michigan. Every design starts with a real stone, a real field fact, or a joke you only get if you have done it. Charts get checked against reliable references before they are printed.

  • MADEPrinted in the US when you order. No warehouse, no leftover stock.
