## Where the size steps do not fully cover the extra cost

Profit per item compared with S to XL (positive means the size earns more):

- Tees, 4XL: about $2.36 less (cost rises $8.36, price rises $6).
- Embroidered Hoodie: 2XL $0.22 less, 3XL $0.45 less, 4XL $0.69 less, 5XL $2.91 less.
- Shoreline Hoodie: 2XL $0.25 less; 3XL to 5XL earn slightly more.
- All other sizes earn the same or slightly more.

Every size still makes money before shipping and fees. Brian chose simple, even steps over matching each cost exactly.
