# Heavy Pockets Co. | Image Standards v2 (proposed)
## Approved visual roles
1. Pixel art: mascot, interfaces, scenic hero, badges. Clean intentional pixel grid with sharp pixel edges; avoid blurred scaling, inconsistent resolutions, and faux 3D effects.
2. Mineral photography: realistic unpolished stones, appropriate to specific species. Neutral light upper left, compact three-quarter framing, generous clear background, restrained believable shadows, and correct scale.
3. Apparel mockups: honest product shape and print placement; final product listings must use real supplier proofs/samples or accurately labeled mockups.

## Species checks
- **Petoskey stone:** Fossil coral (Hexagonaria) cells are flush within gray/tan limestone; varied and incomplete, not stamped identical raised hexagons. Dry marks soft, wet marks clearer.
- **Lake Superior agate:** Outer matrix/skin often covers much of stone; subtle interrupted banding; sometimes ugly, dull, or pitted. No giant pristine bright cartoon bands on every specimen.
- **Leland Blue:** Weathered industrial slag with naturally uneven tiny pores and blue/blue-gray tones; no exaggerated fantasy swirls or perfect repeated bubbles.
- **Red jasper:** Opaque brick-red, dense, subdued mottling; perhaps a thin vein, never automatic translucence.
- **Basalt:** Dense charcoal gray, very fine-grained, gently worn; do not invent holes to signal “volcanic.”
- **Chert:** Fine-grained pale gray/tan, subtle waxy material quality under dry surface; not gritty, cracked, or banded for effect.
- **Puddingstone:** Conglomerate with believable distinct pebbles in matrix; correct relative hardness, wear, and boundaries. Avoid arbitrarily pasted floating stones.

## Dry-to-wet identity lock
Preserve exact silhouette, orientation, markings, nicks, pore locations, camera perspective, canvas proportions, and placement. Apply modest saturation/contrast changes, reduce dusty haze, add local highlights *consistent with existing light direction*. Do not add glow to the perimeter, a glass-like coating, or wet water droplets everywhere. Wet Petoskey markings are more legible without adding new cells.

## Reject immediately
Repeated geometric texture, polygon meshes, obvious AI swirls, symmetrical “too perfect” specimen, marble-like fantasy agates, false species traits, wet and dry being different rocks, invented product labels or inauthentic provenance.

## Production workflow
Real references -> specimen brief -> original dry image -> geological QA -> locked wet edit -> paired QA -> approve -> export full-resolution masters + web variants -> supplier proof / Shopify preview. Keep descriptive source notes and permission records for every commercial artwork.
