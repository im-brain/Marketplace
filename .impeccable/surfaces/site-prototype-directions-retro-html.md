# Surface brief: retro direction sketch

Scope: a one-page direction sketch at `site/prototype/directions/retro.html`, for Brian to compare against the current prototype on his phone. Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation in this environment). Not the production page; nothing in `index.html` changes until Brian picks.

Audience and job: Great Lakes rockhounds arriving from TikTok and Instagram on phones. They must learn in seconds that this sells rockhound tees and socks (not on sale yet), play with Wet Stone, and leave an email for first dibs.

Pinned by Brian (Nov 3, 2026): a 1990s personal-homepage world inspired by cameronsworld.net and a 90s-browser parody screenshot. Keep "Get first dibs" (not a guestbook). All art original and drawn in code; no GeoCities GIFs, no Windows, Netscape, or Twitter names or marks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The store is Brian's own 1998-style home page, honestly under construction, where the one thing that works perfectly is wetting a stone. It refuses the rounded-card Shopify template and the modern "tasteful AI site."

OWN-WORLD: Tiled pixel-pebble beach wallpaper (sand, slate, rust) behind one beveled gray browser window with a navy title bar. Hyperlink blue and visited purple, caution-tape yellow and black, and Lake Superior agate red for the one primary action. Pixel display type (Pixelify Sans), Times-style body (Tinos), raised gray buttons that press in, odometer digits. Night mode: the same window on a dark pixel night beach.

STORY: Visitors see the banner and ticker (what this is), wet the stone (why it's different), see the store is honestly under construction, browse concept designs, pocket a few, and get first dibs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: window chrome with title bar and real nav, the banner (Loaded letters logo over pixel Lake Superior waves), a ticker line naming tees and socks, then the Wet Stone Viewer window with the stone at full width and a raised "Wet it" button. Desktop: the same, with a right sidebar holding Get first dibs and the pocket counter, both visible above the fold.

FORM: Pinned by Brian (90s personal home page), so no roll was run. seed: none (pinned by owner)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
