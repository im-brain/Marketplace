## Needs attention first: the store name

"heavy pockets" has **two live filings, both from the same person (Marques Zahir)**:

- **HEAVY POCKETS**, serial 50027960, Class 16 (printed books on financial education). Filed Aug 3, 2026. A new application, not yet assigned to an examiner.
- **MUN-E HEAVY POCKETS**, serial 99146373, Class 41 (financial literacy podcasts). Filed Apr 21, 2025. An extension of time was granted, which usually means the owner is still working toward showing use.

Neither covers clothing (Class 25), and money books and podcasts are a different market from rock tees. The concern is the future: creators like this often add merch, and a later Class 25 filing from them could get in the way. The older HEAVY POCKETS clothing filing (serial 85912472) is still dead (abandoned 2014).

Suggestion: consider filing a Class 25 application for HEAVY POCKETS CO. soon, once sales start, so the store's own date is on record. A short talk with a trademark attorney is worth it before spending on that. Keep Wet Thumb Co. as the fallback.

## Etsy and other concerns (not trademark issues, but worth knowing)

- **"I lick rocks"** is already on several Etsy tees, including one that says "I LICK ROCKS / AGATES." No filing stops us, but the phrase alone is not original. The lick-mark art has to carry it.
- **"Bands make her dance"** is a goose-hunting slogan (leg bands) already sold as apparel on Etsy, and the original wording is close to the Juicy J song "Bandz A Make Her Dance." The reworded "Agate bands make her dance" is the safer choice.
- **"Sorry I'm late, I saw a rock"** is saturated on Etsy (shirts, stickers, and decals). Keep it as filler.
- **"Agate Expectations"** and **"Will brake for agates"** had no exact Etsy shirt matches. An "I Brake for Agates" license plate exists.
- No search turns up common-law use, so a seller who has used a phrase for years without filing could still have rights in their area.
