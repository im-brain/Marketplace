# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the Heavy Pockets Co. homepage prototype (`site/prototype/index.html`). Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation in this environment).
Audience and job: Great Lakes rockhounds on phones, arriving from TikTok or Instagram. They should understand "tees and socks for rockhounds" at once, wet a stone, pocket designs, and get first dibs.
Proof on hand: none real yet. Products are concepts (the sock is a ChatGPT concept image), stones are SAMPLE placeholders, and there are no prices.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md. No em dashes, Oxford comma, readable dark mode, reduced motion respected, and Wet Stone never on garments. All decoration is original and drawn in code; no Windows, Netscape, Twitter, or GeoCities names, marks, or GIFs.
Memorable moment: wetting the stone in the Wet Stone Viewer.
History: Nov 2 to 3 the homepage was Direction B, "Sorting tray" (kept at `site/prototype/sorting-tray.html`). On Nov 3 Brian said it read too AI and picked the 1990s home page direction, first built as `directions/retro.html`.
Unresolved: real stone photos (Brian's own pairs), email provider, and the print reality of the sock (matte, printed stripes).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The store is Brian's own 1998-style home page, honestly under construction, where the one thing that works perfectly is wetting a stone. It refuses the rounded-card Shopify template and the tasteful modern AI site.
OWN-WORLD: Tiled pixel-pebble beach wallpaper (sand, slate, rust) behind one beveled gray browser window with a navy title bar. Hyperlink blue and visited purple, caution-tape yellow and black, and Lake Superior agate red for the one primary action. A hand-placed pixel wordmark with an orange period, pixel display type (Pixelify Sans), Times-style body (Tinos), raised gray buttons that press in, odometer digits, and 88x31-style badges. Night mode: the same window on a dark pixel beach.
STORY: Banner and ticker say what this is; the Wet Stone Viewer shows why it's different; the Under Construction band is honest that nothing is on sale; the five finds on a pixel water band can be pocketed; first dibs captures the email; Field Notes, the Field Guide, and About the webmaster carry the story.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Window chrome with title bar, real nav, and the Pocket odometer; the pixel wordmark banner over Lake Superior waves with the tagline and the offer line; the ticker; then the Wet Stone Viewer. Desktop: a sticky sidebar holds Get first dibs, fully above 900px. Phone: Get first dibs follows the five finds.
FORM: Pinned by Brian (90s personal home page), so no roll was run. seed: none (pinned by owner)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
