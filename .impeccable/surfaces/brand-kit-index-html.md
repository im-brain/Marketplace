# Surface brief: brand kit comparison page (brand/kit/index.html)

Scope: a one-page decision document showing four brand directions for Heavy Pockets Co. Mode: Persuade (each option must sell itself), read on Brian's iPad and phone, light and dark.
Audience and job: Brian picks one direction (or a hybrid) in a few minutes, then approves it as the Step 4 brand kit.
Constraints: Brian's three pinned options (Field Notebook, Lakeshore Workwear, Wet Stone Minimal) plus the roll's assigned wildcard (Lake Chart). No em dashes, Oxford comma, dark mode readable. Free OFL fonts only.
Roll ran degraded (impeccable.style not on the network allowlist): no challengers, no quality-bar boards.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every color has a dry and a wet state; the page proves the brand by letting you wet it. Refuses the category default of a static logo sheet with swatches.
OWN-WORLD: Each option section is its own world (specimen-label gray and slate; duck canvas, navy, and chain stitch; dull dry gray with one glossy wet red; NOAA chart white, shallow blue, buff land, and magenta). Shared page chrome is quiet slate ink on Petoskey gray.
STORY: Brian sees the shared wet/dry idea, then each option as wordmark, palette, type, tee, and phone, reads its honest risk, and replies with a letter.
