## Return and refund policy

Every Heavy Pockets Co. item is printed after you order it, just for you. Because of that, we can't take returns for a change of mind or a wrong size. We do fix every problem that is our fault.

**We will replace or refund your order if:**
- The print is flawed, faded, misaligned, or missing.
- The item arrives damaged.
- You received the wrong item, size, or color.
- The package is lost in transit.

**How to ask:**
1. Email hello@heavypocketsco.com within 30 days of the delivery date.
2. Include your order number and a clear photo showing the problem. For a wrong item, show the item and its label.
3. We reply within [2 business days] with a replacement or a refund, your choice.

You do not need to ship the item back. Please keep it, or donate it.

**Sizing:** Each product page lists the sizes. Please check them before you order. If you order the wrong size, we can't exchange it, but email us anyway. We'll see if we can help.

**Refunds** go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take [5 to 10 business days] to show them.

**Cancellations and address changes:** Orders go to print quickly, usually within [24 hours]. Email us as soon as you can and we'll try, but we can't promise a change after printing starts.

## Privacy policy

Last updated: [date of launch]

Heavy Pockets Co. ("we," "us") runs heavypocketsco.com. This page explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

**What we collect**
- **When you order:** your name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number if you give one, and what you ordered.
- **Payment details:** these go straight to our payment processor (Shopify Payments or another processor you choose at checkout). We never see or store your full card number.
- **Mailing list:** your email address, if you sign up for updates or to be first in line for drops.
- **Visit data:** basic technical information such as your device, browser, pages viewed, and approximate location, collected by our store platform and through cookies it needs to run your cart and checkout.
- **Messages:** anything you send to hello@heavypocketsco.com.

**How we use it**
- To make, ship, and support your order.
- To send order and shipping updates.
- To send our newsletter, only if you signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- To prevent fraud and keep the store working.
- To meet tax and legal requirements.

**Who handles it for us** (they receive only what they need):
- Shopify, which runs the store, checkout, and customer records.
- Printify and the print partner that makes and ships your order. They get your name, address, and what you ordered.
- Our payment processor.
- Carriers that deliver your package.

**We do not sell your personal information.**

**Your choices**
- Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom.
- Email hello@heavypocketsco.com to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. We'll reply within [30 days]. We may need to keep order records for tax and legal reasons.
- You can block cookies in your browser, but the cart and checkout may not work.

**Children:** The store isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

**Changes:** If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.

**Contact:** hello@heavypocketsco.com
