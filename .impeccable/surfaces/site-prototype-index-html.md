# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the Heavy Pockets Co. homepage prototype (`site/prototype/index.html`). Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: Great Lakes rockhounds on phones, arriving from TikTok or Instagram. They should understand "tees and socks for rockhounds" at once, play with one stone, and tap Shop.
Proof on hand: none real yet. Products are concepts, stones are SAMPLE placeholders, and there are no prices.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md. No em dashes, Oxford comma, readable dark mode, reduced motion respected, and Wet Stone never on garments.
Memorable moment: wetting the big stone in its compartment.
Unresolved: real photos (Unsplash and Pexels pages are blocked to scripts, so the official Unsplash API key is pending from Brian), final palette and fonts (become the Step 4 brand kit on approval), and email provider.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the tackle box you empty your pockets into after a beach day, with one find per compartment. It refuses the hero, carousel, and equal-card storefront, and it refuses a uniform bento grid. Compartments are uneven, like a real molded tray.
OWN-WORLD: Molded cobalt plastic is the ground. Wells are recessed with soft inner shadow and divider lips. Safety-orange latch color is reserved for the primary action and the cart. White type. Display is a fat, rounded, molded face, and the body is a clean grotesk. Stones and concept art sit in wells like objects. The menu is the smoked clear lid closing over the tray.
STORY: In one line, they get what's sold and who it's for. They wet a stone and see that a real rockhound made this. They open a product compartment or tap Shop. Lower down, the page leaves the tray for an open-lid editorial spread (field note, Field Guide) and then returns to a small tray for signup and footer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Lid strip with the wordmark on the left and Shop, Field Guide, Menu, and an orange Cart on the right. Below it, an uneven tray: a wide left well holds "Head down. Pockets full.", the offer line, and an orange Shop button; a tall well holds the Wet Stone; two narrow wells hold small stones; product wells peek at the bottom edge. On mobile: the stone well, then the headline well, with Shop inside 844px.
FORM: Impeccable's pick (my #1 of 7), chosen by Brian over the roll's assigned #6 (rock club bulletin). seed:cd0bde1d843a47dc. Signature interaction: Wet Stone. Motion grammar: objects settle into wells and the lid slides.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
