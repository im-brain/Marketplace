## Users

Great Lakes rockhounds in Michigan, Wisconsin, and Minnesota: people who walk beaches head down looking for Lake Superior agates, Petoskey stones, and beach glass. **[inferred]** Most find the store on TikTok or Instagram, on a phone, often in the evening or on the beach in bright light. They buy a tee or socks as an identity piece and as gifts for other rockhounds.

## Product Purpose

A print-on-demand store selling original tees and socks for rockhounds. Success: profit greater than Brian's monthly AI bill (about $20), then greater than all fixed costs (about 6 orders a month).

## Positioning

"Wet Stone": every rockhound wets a stone to see its true color. The store makes that the brand idea ("Dry rocks lie"), the site's signature interaction (dry images "wet" on hover or tap), and the art (for example, a dull stone with one glossy lick mark). Every design starts from a real Great Lakes find and carries a field note about it. Generic rock-pun shops cannot claim this.

## Operating Context

- Product-only social video (no face), ideally the real stone held next to the shirt drawn from it.
- Free field guide on the site; each chart links to its shirt.
- Printify prints and ships; there is no inventory.

## Brand Commitments

- Writing: no em dashes, ever. Always the Oxford comma. Concise and defensible.
- HTML must be readable in dark mode.
- Voice: warm, dry humor, a fellow rockhound, not a corporate brand. **[inferred]**
- The lake palette named in PLAN.md Step 4: Superior slate, agate red, Petoskey gray, and sand.

## Product Principles

1. Truth first: every stone label and fact is verified by Brian before it goes on a product.
2. Every design starts with a real find.
3. Original over clever: a pun only ships if the art makes it ours.
4. Cheap to run: no paid ads at launch, tools under $75 a month.
