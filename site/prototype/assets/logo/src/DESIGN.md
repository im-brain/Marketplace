# Loaded letters source notes

2026-11-02. Selected by Brian as logo direction A.

The image-generation concept in `../options/loaded-letters-concept.png` is a reference, not the final lettering. The finished SVGs use clean paths and require no fonts. Bagel Fat One supplied the starting shapes. The H and P were redrawn, lower curves were weighted and flattened slightly, spacing was fitted, Co. was reduced, and the final period became a rounded square. The HP mark is a separate custom shared-stem drawing, with no stone icon.

Bagel Fat One source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bagelfatone. License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included here. The font itself is not bundled.

`hp-wordmark.svg` is the horizontal web wordmark. `hp-stacked.svg` is the square identity. `hp-wordmark-1c.svg` is a bolder stacked production version, because the full horizontal name becomes too small for reliable one-inch embroidery. It uses filled paths and currentColor only. The 1.2pt requirement equals 0.423mm on the finished piece. The broader stems are designed above that threshold at one inch wide, but a supplier stitch proof is still required. Use the HP mark for tiny cuffs and tags. Do not shrink the full horizontal name into a favicon.

The header is checked separately at 320px. Its SVG scales down while the link remains at least 44px tall. Use currentColor when placing a master on a light or dark background. The website wordmark is white. The proof sheet also checks dark ink on the light lid, white on black, and white on charcoal. No garment or product photographs were generated.

The social card uses an AI-generated empty tray background with the exact vector logo and outlined tagline composited over it. It contains no AI specimens or products. Replace the tray background only if a photographed tray is preferred.
