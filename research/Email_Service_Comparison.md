# Email service for the signup list (Nov 7, 2026)

Website to-do 8. Prices come from third-party pricing guides found by web search on Nov 7, 2026, not from each company's own page. Re-check before signing up.

| Service | Free plan | Notes for Heavy Pockets |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify Email (now "Shopify Messaging") | 10,000 emails a month, included with any paid Shopify plan; about $1 per 1,000 after | Comes with Shopify at Step 12. Built-in unsubscribe links. Nothing extra to pay or connect. |
| Kit (formerly ConvertKit) | Up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited broadcasts, Kit branding | Most generous free plan. Best choice if Brian wants to email before Shopify. One source says 1,000 subscribers; most say 10,000. |
| MailerLite | 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month (cut in mid-2026 from 500) | Shrinking free plan; not recommended. |
| Klaviyo | 250 active profiles, 500 emails a month | Strong with Shopify, but the free plan is small and paid starts at $20 a month. |
| Buttondown | 100 subscribers | Simple, but the smallest free plan. |

## Recommendation

Don't sign up for anything yet. The page promises one email, when the store opens, and the list has 2 people. Keep collecting in Cloudflare (free, already working). At Step 12, export the list and import it into Shopify Email for the launch email. If Brian wants to send anything before then (for example, sample photos), use Kit's free plan.

## Before the first email (any service)

- **Mailing address:** U.S. law (CAN-SPAM) requires a valid postal address in every marketing email. To keep a home address private, use a P.O. box or a virtual mailbox.
- **Unsubscribe:** every service above adds the link automatically, which keeps the page's promise.
- **Import:** mark imported people as having agreed to emails; they signed up on heavypocketsco.com.
